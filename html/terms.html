Introduction

Welcome to the Barangay Sillon Community Complaint and Response System (BSCCARS). By registering and using this system, you agree to follow these Terms of Service. These terms are designed to keep the service fair, safe, and effective for all residents and authorized personnel.

How to Use BSCCARS

The following guidelines apply to all registered users:

  1. Complaint Limit. Residents may have up to 5 active complaints at a time. Active complaints are those that are still pending or in progress. Once a complaint is resolved, you may submit a new one.
  2. Recurring Issues. If you have a continuing or repetitive concern, add a follow-up to the existing complaint rather than filing a duplicate.
  3. Proper Use. Use the system honestly. Filing false, abusive, or harassing complaints may be reviewed by authorized barangay personnel and handled through appropriate barangay procedures.
  4. Language. You may describe complaints in English, Cebuano, Tagalog, or Bisaya so you can explain your issue clearly.
  5. Attachments. You may attach one image (JPG, JPEG, or PNG, up to 5 MB) and one video (MP4, up to 10 MB) per complaint. Videos must be 20 seconds or shorter. Attach only content that relates to your complaint. Do not upload unlawful content or private content of other people that is not necessary for the complaint. You must have the right or authority to submit the file.
  6. Confidentiality. If you mark a complaint confidential, your identity will be hidden from the Public Feed and from other residents, but authorized administrators can still see it as needed to process the complaint. The complaint title is always visible in the Public Feed, so do not put names or sensitive details in it.
  7. Information About Other People. For Money Debt complaints, you must provide the respondent's details accurately and only for a genuine dispute. Respondent details are used only for barangay processing and Katarungang Pambarangay documents.
  8. Data Privacy. Your information is collected for complaint processing and service administration only. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Account Responsibilities

You are responsible for safeguarding your account credentials and for the accuracy of the information you submit through BSCCARS. Verify your email address after registration, because password recovery works only with a verified email. Notify the barangay office immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

Registration and Approval

Registrations are verified by barangay staff using the valid ID you upload. Approval is required before your account becomes active, so submitting a registration form does not provide immediate access to BSCCARS. Review is done during office hours (8:00 AM to 5:00 PM). Residents must be at least 18 years old to register their own account. If a minor has a concern or complaint, a parent or legal guardian should submit the complaint using the parent or guardian's own account. That account holder is the complainant of record; provide only the minor's information needed for the barangay to assess and respond to the concern.

Name and Identity Records

Your name and identity details are treated as records verified against your ID and cannot be edited in the system. To correct them, visit the Barangay Office with a valid ID. You may update your email address (which requires your current password and verification of the new address) and your profile photo yourself.

Use of Your Submissions

By submitting a complaint, you allow Barangay Sillon to use the information for complaint processing, mediation and Katarungang Pambarangay proceedings, record-keeping, and summary reports. Complaints are archived rather than deleted so that an audit trail is preserved.

System Access

BSCCARS access is limited to verified residents and authorized barangay personnel. Resident applications require approval by barangay staff, and duplicate or invalid applications may be rejected. Resident records and complaints may be archived or restored for record-keeping. Administrator accounts may be activated or deactivated by the Super Admin as required by official staffing and security needs. Barangay personnel may update a complaint's status, archive it, or decline applications as needed under barangay procedures.
