Introduction

Welcome to the Barangay Sillon Community Complaint and Response System (BSCCARS). By registering and using this system, you agree to follow these Terms of Service. These terms are designed to keep the service fair, safe, and effective for all residents and authorized personnel.

How to Use BSCCARS

The following guidelines apply to all registered users:

  1. Complaint Limit. Residents may have up to 5 active complaints at a time. Active complaints are those that are still pending or in progress. Once a complaint is resolved, you may submit a new one.
  2. Recurring Issues. If you have a continuing or repetitive concern, update the existing complaint rather than filing a duplicate.
  3. Proper Use. Use the system honestly. Filing false or abusive complaints may result in account restrictions or review by the Barangay Captain.
  4. Language. You may describe complaints in English, Cebuano, Tagalog, or Bisaya so you can explain your issue clearly.
  5. Confidentiality. If you mark a complaint confidential, your identity will be hidden from public listings, but authorized administrators may still review the information as needed.
  6. Data Privacy. Your information is collected for complaint processing and service administration only. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Account Responsibilities

You are responsible for safeguarding your account credentials. You are also responsible for the accuracy of the information you submit through BSCCARS. Notify the barangay office immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

Registration and Approval

New registrations are subject to verification by barangay staff. Approval is required before your account becomes active. Registration does not guarantee instant access.

System Access and Suspension

The Barangay Admin may suspend or deactivate accounts that violate these terms, misuse the system, or file repeated false or malicious complaints.

Disclaimer

BSCCARS is a community complaint management tool. It does not replace official barangay processes, legal advice, or emergency services.
