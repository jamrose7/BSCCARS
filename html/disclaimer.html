General Disclaimer

BSCCARS is provided by Barangay Sillon as a tool for submitting and tracking community complaints, notices, updates, and related barangay response activity. The system is intended to support barangay operations, but it does not guarantee outcomes or replace official barangay procedures.

Not for Emergencies

BSCCARS is not an emergency reporting service. For urgent threats, crimes in progress, medical emergencies, fires, or immediate safety concerns, contact the appropriate emergency responders or the Barangay Sillon office directly. Complaints marked High Priority are flagged for urgent review but are not monitored around the clock.

Office Hours and Response Times

Barangay staff act on complaints and registrations during office hours (8:00 AM to 5:00 PM). Barangay Sillon aims to review and update complaints within 3 days, but this is a target and not a guarantee. Response times may be longer because of staffing, holidays, or the nature of the complaint.

Users are responsible for the accuracy of the information they submit, including details about other people. The barangay is not responsible for incorrect, incomplete, or misleading information provided through the system.

Public and Confidential Complaints

Complaint summaries that are not archived may be displayed in the Public Feed for community awareness, including the complaint title. Confidential complaints hide the complainant's identity from public listings, but authorized barangay personnel may still review the full details when needed to process the complaint. Do not put sensitive personal details in a complaint title.

System Updates and Notices

Complaint statuses and in-app or email notifications are administrative updates to help users follow barangay action. They are not legal advice, legal findings, or guarantees that a specific result will occur.

Official Katarungang Pambarangay Forms

Hearing notices and other Katarungang Pambarangay (KP) forms are prepared in BSCCARS for use by the Punong Barangay, Lupon, and Pangkat. Once printed, signed, and served by the proper barangay officials, these forms are official barangay documents governed by the Local Government Code and KP rules. The in-app notifications about them are for information only.
