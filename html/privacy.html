Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Our Commitment

Barangay Sillon is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, and its Implementing Rules and Regulations. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights as a data subject.

Information We Collect

When you register and use BSCCARS, we collect the following information:

  • Identity details: full name and date of birth (the date of birth is used only to confirm that you are at least 18 years old)
  • Residency details: your purok within Barangay Sillon
  • Contact details: email address and contact number (used for account verification, notifications, and barangay follow-up)
  • Valid government-issued ID: an image uploaded during registration, used only to verify that you are a resident of Barangay Sillon
  • Profile photo: optional, uploaded by you from your profile page
  • Complaint information: title, category, description, incident date and time, purok, confidentiality choice, optional image or video attachments, resident follow-up updates, and status updates
  • Information about other people: for Money Debt complaints, the name, purok, and (optionally) contact number of the other party (the respondent)
  • System and account records: the uploaded government ID image, optional profile photo, email-verification and pending email-change records, password-reset and administrator-activation token records (tokens are stored as hashes), account activity logs (such as sign-ins, complaints, and administrative actions), notification records, administrator responses and complaint comments, resident follow-ups, and hearing notice/proceeding details

Information About Other People

If you file a Money Debt complaint, you will be asked to provide details about the respondent. Please provide only information you have a legitimate reason to share and that is accurate to the best of your knowledge. Respondent details are used only by authorized barangay personnel to process the complaint and prepare Katarungang Pambarangay documents. They are never shown in the Public Feed. If a complaint involves a minor, provide only the details necessary for the barangay to act on it.

How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used only for barangay complaint processing, response management, and system administration. This includes the following:

  • Verifying your residency and eligibility to use BSCCARS
  • Creating and managing your account
  • Submitting, tracking, and resolving complaints
  • Preparing and recording hearing notices and mediation proceedings
  • Communicating complaint updates and notification alerts
  • Providing support for account or complaint issues
  • Improving system security and maintaining audit records
  • Managing resident application approval, rejection, archiving, or restoration when needed for official barangay records
  • Producing summary reports for barangay record-keeping and decision-making

We process your data based on your consent given at registration and on the barangay's lawful duty to receive and act on community complaints. The barangay should document the applicable lawful basis for each processing purpose before deployment.

Email Verification and Notifications

After registration we send a verification link to your email address. A verified email address is needed to recover your password. We also send email notifications for account events (verification, password reset, email change) and for complaint status changes and official responses. Hearing notice updates are shown in the system as in-app notifications.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or trade your personal data. Information is shared only with authorized Barangay Sillon officials and staff who need it to handle complaints, verify registrations, send notices, maintain records, or administer the system. Disclosure may also occur when required by law, court order, or public safety needs.

Service Providers

BSCCARS sends emails through the SMTP/email provider configured by the barangay. Account emails may contain account-related links; complaint update emails may contain your name, complaint number, title, status, or an official response. The provider receives the information needed to deliver each email and processes it under its own terms and privacy practices. Administrator report pages load a PDF-generation library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com; that service may receive technical information from your browser, such as its IP address, when the library is requested.

Public Complaint Information

Complaints that are not archived may appear in the Public Feed, which is visible to signed-in users, so residents can follow community concerns. The Public Feed is limited to summary information: complaint number, title, category, purok, incident date and time, status, and your display name when the complaint is not confidential. Full complaint details, attachments, respondent information, and administrative notes are not shown in the Public Feed.

Important: the complaint title is always visible in the Public Feed, including for confidential complaints. Do not include names, addresses, contact numbers, or other sensitive details in the title.

Confidential Complaints

If you choose to hide your identity, your name is omitted from the Public Feed and other residents' complaint views. The complaint title, number, category, purok, incident date and time, and status can still be visible to signed-in users. Choose a title that does not identify you or another person. Authorized administrators can see your identity to process the complaint and prepare official hearing documents. BSCCARS does not offer fully anonymous complaints.

Cookies and Local Storage

BSCCARS does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To keep you signed in, your browser stores a sign-in token and basic account details in local storage until you sign out or the session expires. If you tick "Remember me", your email address is also stored in your browser. A registration draft may be kept temporarily in your browser session. Use "Sign Out" on shared computers.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include password hashing, role-based access control, protected file access, and activity logging. Access is limited to authorized personnel who require it for official barangay duties. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Breach Notification

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, the barangay will notify affected users and the National Privacy Commission within the periods required by RA 10173 and NPC rules.

Data Retention

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for its stated purposes or as required by applicable law and records-retention rules. BSCCARS allows authorized administrators to archive and restore resident records and complaints. Archiving removes a record from ordinary active lists, but does not permanently delete it; archived records remain stored and may remain accessible to authorized administrators. Any retention or disposal of records must follow the applicable legal and barangay records-management requirements.

Your Rights

Under RA 10173, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right to be informed — to know how your data is collected and used
  • Right to access — to request a copy of your personal data held by BSCCARS
  • Right to correction — to correct inaccurate or incomplete information. Your name and identity details are verified against your ID, so they cannot be edited in the system. To correct them, visit the Barangay Office with a valid ID. You can update your email address and profile photo yourself from your profile page.
  • Right to erasure or blocking — to request erasure or blocking where applicable. The barangay will assess each request under applicable law and its records obligations; archiving a record does not itself erase it.
  • Right to object — to object to processing of your personal data
  • Right to data portability — to obtain a structured copy of your data
  • Right to damages — to claim compensation where your data was inaccurately, unlawfully, or without authority used
  • Right to lodge a complaint — to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC)

Exercising Your Rights and Contacting Us

To exercise your rights or ask about this policy, contact the Barangay Sillon office or email barangaysillon593@gmail.com. Include enough information for staff to understand the request. The barangay may ask for reasonable proof of identity before disclosing or changing personal information. Requests will be handled within the periods required by applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
