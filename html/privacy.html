Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Our Commitment

Barangay Sillon is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, and its Implementing Rules and Regulations. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a data subject.

Information We Collect

When you register and use BSCCARS, we collect the following information:

  • Full name, date of birth, and address
  • Purok within Barangay Sillon
  • Contact number and email address
  • Verification documents, including a valid government-issued ID
  • Complaint details, including category, description, attachments, and status updates
  • Account activity logs, such as submitted complaints and system actions

How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used only for barangay complaint processing, response management, and system administration. This includes the following:

  • Verifying your residency and eligibility to use BSCCARS
  • Creating and managing your account
  • Submitting, tracking, and resolving complaints
  • Communicating complaint updates and notification alerts
  • Providing support for account or complaint issues
  • Improving system security and maintaining audit records

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or trade your personal data. Information is shared only with authorized Barangay Sillon officials and staff who need it to handle complaints. Disclosure may also occur when required by law, court order, or public safety needs.

Confidential Complaints

If you choose to submit a confidential complaint, your identity will be hidden from public listings and feeds. Authorized administrators may still access your information when necessary for investigation and resolution.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access is limited to authorized personnel who require it for official barangay duties.

Data Retention

Personal data is retained only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by applicable laws and barangay records management policies. Records may be kept for legal, audit, or administrative reasons even after a complaint is resolved.

Your Rights

Under RA 10173, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right to be informed — to know how your data is collected and used
  • Right to access — to request a copy of your personal data held by BSCCARS
  • Right to correction — to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to erasure — to request deletion of your data, subject to legal requirements
  • Right to object — to object to processing of your personal data
  • Right to data portability — to obtain a structured copy of your data
  • Right to lodge a complaint — to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC)

Exercising Your Rights

To exercise your rights, contact the Barangay Sillon office or email barangaysillon593@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time in accordance with RA 10173 and NPC guidelines.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For privacy-related concerns, please email barangaysillon593@gmail.com or visit the Barangay Sillon office.
